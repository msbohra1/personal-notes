- [Post by Tirthankar "TC" Chakraborty on LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): Argues satellite-derived land surface temperature (LST) is routinely misread as air temperature, overstating heatwave severity and tree-shade cooling benefits in urban heat island coverage; LST is a "skin" temperature of rooftops/asphalt/canopies, not what people feel in the air. Relevant to [[Remote_Sensing]] data limitations and [[Climate_Change]]/heatwave reporting.
