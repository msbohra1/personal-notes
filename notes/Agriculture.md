- [Agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): [[Python]] toolkit for delineating agricultural field boundaries from satellite imagery, combining geospatial foundation models (Delineate-Anything, DINOv3, Prithvi-EO-2.0) with [[Remote_Sensing]] data from Sentinel-2, Landsat, and NAIP. Keywords: field boundary delineation, satellite imagery, foundation models, precision agriculture, Google Earth Engine, Python
